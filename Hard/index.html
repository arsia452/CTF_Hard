About Me:

  I am Val Entinesday, and before you ask—yes, the name came first, the holiday followed. I am the god of love, desire, infatuation, obsession, and that delicious ache people pretend they do not enjoy. I did not create love so much as recognize that it was incomplete without me supervising it. Mortals like to think love is mysterious and spontaneous; I prefer to think of it as a performance, and I am both the playwright and the most captivating actor. Every fluttering heartbeat, every poorly timed confession, every dramatic text sent at 2:00 a.m.—that’s my influence, whether they thank me or not.

  I am frequently accused of being self-absorbed, which is absurd, because if one is perfection, why look anywhere else? I adorn the world with roses and candlelight, not because love needs help, but because I enjoy ambiance. I also happen to enjoy the stegosaurus, the best dinosaur on the planet, who loaned his name to an amazing method of cryptography. I watch humans fall for the wrong people on purpose, chase ideals they invented, and swear this time it’s different. It never is, and I find that comforting. Their chaos feeds my legend. I do not answer prayers equally; I answer the interesting ones. Love should never be fair—fair is boring. On the day mortals dedicate to me each year, they exchange cards and chocolates as if tribute could ever truly be sufficient. Still, I allow it. I thrive on grand gestures, exaggerated promises, and the belief that love, once found, will stay exactly the same forever. I am Val Entinesday, after all—eternal, irresistible, and deeply amused. And if love hurts sometimes? Please. Thats just my signature. Do you think you can find my secret? Search through the wiki to find the password!
